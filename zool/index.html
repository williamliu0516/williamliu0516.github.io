Zool Support

Zool plays the films and series on your own storage and media servers, in Dolby Vision and HDR where your screen supports them, and builds a poster wall from what it finds. It works with WebDAV and SMB servers, and with Jellyfin, Emby and Plex.

Email: support@xiaweiliu.com

Privacy policy: xiaweiliu.com/zool/privacy

Asking for help

Write to support@xiaweiliu.com and say what you did, what happened, and the exact message the app showed (a screenshot is fine). It helps to attach a diagnostics report: in Settings, open Diagnostics and choose Share Report. The report leaves out passwords, user names, tokens and keys; what it contains is listed in the privacy policy. Never send your password.

Common questions

The app cannot reach my server

  • Check the address: https:// or http://, the port, and for WebDAV the path the service lives at, for example https://nas.example.com:5006.
  • Check the user name, the password and the folder. Many servers treat upper and lower case as different.
  • On iPhone and iPad, Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network > Zool must be on. On a Mac, the same switch is in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network.
  • Away from home, the server must be reachable from the internet, or connect to your own VPN first.
  • If the app says the server’s certificate isn’t trusted (common with a self-signed certificate), it shows the certificate’s fingerprint. Choose Trust This Certificate only if the fingerprint matches the one your server shows.

“The server can only send whole files”

The app reads videos a part at a time, which needs a server that supports range requests. Most NAS systems and WebDAV servers do; if yours does not, try another WebDAV service on the same storage.

Slow start, slow seeking or pauses to buffer

The app reads the file straight from your server, so start-up and seeking depend on the connection between your device and the server. A 4K remux can need well over 50 Mbps at its peaks: check your server’s upload speed when you watch away from home, and your Wi-Fi (5 GHz or Ethernet is better than 2.4 GHz). A VPN or a distant server adds a delay to every seek.

How should I name my folders?

  • One folder per film or series, named with the title and year, for example Big Buck Bunny (2008).
  • Series can keep episodes in season folders (Season 1, S01); episode numbers are read from names such as S01E02.
  • An NFO file (movie.nfo, tvshow.nfo) with a TMDB or IMDb ID settles the match, and posters stored beside your files are used before TMDB’s.
  • Disc images (.iso) and Blu-ray folders play their main feature, without menus. DVD folders do not play.

A title has the wrong poster or name

Open the title, or the menu on its card, and choose Match… to search TMDB and pick the right entry; your pick stays through later scans. Remove Match leaves the folder unmatched, and Match Automatically hands it back to the app. Renaming the folder to the title and year, then choosing Rescan, also works.

It plays in HDR10, not Dolby Vision

“HDR10 (DV source)” means the file is Dolby Vision, but the screen, or something between the device and the screen, does not take Dolby Vision, so it plays in HDR10. An external TV must support Dolby Vision itself, as must every adapter or receiver in between. Dolby Vision Profile 7 (from UHD Blu-ray) plays as Profile 8.1.

My receiver shows Dolby Digital Plus instead of TrueHD

Apple devices cannot send TrueHD to a receiver unchanged, in any app. TrueHD tracks, TrueHD Atmos included, play as Dolby Digital Plus 5.1, without height channels; the playback info panel shows what happens to each track. Dolby Digital Plus tracks with Atmos play with Atmos.

Subtitles

The app shows subtitles inside the video file, text and picture (PGS) alike, and subtitle files stored beside the video (for example Film.en.srt, or in a Subs folder).

Does removing a source delete my files?

No. Removing a source only takes its titles off the wall. Deleting a video from inside the app does delete it on the server, and the app asks you to confirm first.

常见问题

连不上服务器

  • 检查地址：https:// 或 http://、端口，WebDAV 还要有服务所在的路径，例如 https://nas.example.com:5006。
  • 检查用户名、密码和文件夹。很多服务器区分大小写。
  • iPhone 和 iPad 上要打开“设置 > 隐私与安全性 > 本地网络 > Zool”；Mac 上同样的开关在“系统设置 > 隐私与安全性 > 本地网络”。
  • 在外面时，服务器要能从外网访问，或者先连上你自己的 VPN。
  • 如果 app 提示服务器的证书不受信任（自签名证书常见这种情况），它会显示证书指纹。只有指纹和你服务器上显示的一致时，才选择“信任这个证书”。

提示“服务器只能整个发送文件”

app 分段读取视频，需要服务器支持范围请求（Range）。大多数 NAS 和 WebDAV 服务器都支持；如果你的不支持，可以在同一个存储上换一个 WebDAV 服务。

起播慢、拖动慢，或者停下来缓冲

app 直接从你的服务器读取文件，起播和拖动的速度取决于设备和服务器之间的网络。4K REMUX 的峰值码率可能远超 50 Mbps： 在外面看时检查服务器的上行速度，也检查你所在的 Wi-Fi（5 GHz 或有线比 2.4 GHz 好）。VPN 或离得很远的服务器会让每次拖动都多等一会儿。

文件夹怎么命名？

  • 每部电影或剧集一个文件夹，用片名加年份命名，例如 Big Buck Bunny (2008)。
  • 剧集可以分季文件夹（Season 1、S01），集号从 S01E02 这样的名字里读出。
  • 带 TMDB 或 IMDb 编号的 NFO 文件（movie.nfo、tvshow.nfo）直接决定匹配；放在文件旁边的海报优先于 TMDB 的海报。
  • 光盘镜像（.iso）和蓝光文件夹只播正片，不播菜单。DVD 文件夹不能播放。

海报或片名不对

打开这部作品，或者它卡片上的菜单，选择“匹配…”，在 TMDB 里搜索并选对条目；选好的条目以后扫描都不会改。“取消匹配”让这个文件夹不匹配任何条目， “自动匹配”交还给 app 自动处理。把文件夹改名为片名加年份再“重新扫描”，也可以。

播放的是 HDR10，不是杜比视界

“HDR10（杜比视界片源）”表示文件是杜比视界，但屏幕、或者设备和屏幕之间的某个环节不支持杜比视界，于是按 HDR10 播放。 外接电视本身要支持杜比视界，中间的转接头和功放也都要支持。杜比视界 Profile 7（来自 UHD 蓝光）按 Profile 8.1 播放。

功放显示 Dolby Digital Plus，而不是 TrueHD

Apple 设备在任何 app 里都不能把 TrueHD 原样送给功放。TrueHD 音轨（包括 TrueHD 全景声）按 Dolby Digital Plus 5.1 播放，没有天空声道； 播放信息面板会显示每条音轨怎样处理。带全景声的 Dolby Digital Plus 音轨按全景声播放。

字幕

app 显示视频文件里的字幕（文字字幕和图片字幕 PGS），也会使用视频旁边的字幕文件（例如 Film.zh.srt，或者 Subs 文件夹里的）。

移除片源会删除我的文件吗？

不会。移除片源只是把它的作品从海报墙上拿掉。在 app 里删除视频则会删除服务器上的文件，删除前 app 会请你确认。
